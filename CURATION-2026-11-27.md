by path, no timestamps, `canon_version` read from `VERSION`.

Schema: `{schema_version, canon_version, total_entries, documents[{path, uid,
title, tags}]}`. UID and tags are empty when a document has no front-matter, which
is expected for standards that carry `title`/`version` only.

## Why the deferred paths are deferred

The four deferred standards files and one dataset either remove data from an
active contract or restate it. `GRIDCORE-STANDARDS.json` (−71) and
`USX-STANDARDS.json` (−19) feed the Prose/GridCore content contract in uCore;
accepting a net removal without the owner's review could silently change palette
or token semantics. They stay in the candidate branch and can be taken in a later
tranche with a per-file diff review.
